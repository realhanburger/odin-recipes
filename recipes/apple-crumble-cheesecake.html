Apple Crumble Cheesecake

Probably the best cheesecake I've ever made!

Ingredients

  • 3 apples
  • 300g soft cheese
  • 5g caster sugar
  • corn flour
  • 100g plain flour
  • 1 medium egg
  • vanilla extract
  • 100g butter
  • 80g yoghurt
  • 120g double cream
  • ground cinnamon
  • 150g biscoff

Instructions

  1. To make apple sauce: Cube 3 apples, add to a pan with 30g sugar, half of a lemon juice, and a bit of ground cinnamon and corn starch. Turn the heat off when the apples are a bit tender.
  2. To make the crumble: Soften 60g butter and mix it with 100g flour and 40g sugar. Till there is no big butter lumps.
  3. To make cheesecake batter: Mix an egg, 300g cream cheese, 75g sugar, half of a lemon juice, vanilla extract, 5g corn starch, 80g yoghurt and 120g double cream together till it's smooth.
  4. To make the base: Crush 150g biscoff and add 40g melted butter in it. Place the mixture in a cake tin and use a glass to press it flat.
  5. After all of the above has been prepared, add cheesecake batter, then apple sauce, then the crumble in the cake tin. Preheat the oven and put the cake in for 45 mins at 180 degrees.
